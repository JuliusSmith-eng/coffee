About

Our own roast, our own rules

Black Coffee started as a small roastery in a basement — today it's a place people come to not just for coffee, but for quiet, conversation, or a page of a book. We buy beans directly from farmers, roast them ourselves, and never hold a batch longer than two weeks.

Our baristas don't just pour coffee — they remember how you take it.
